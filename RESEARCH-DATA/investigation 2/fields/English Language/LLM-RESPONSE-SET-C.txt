A3c) Identify unclear or awkward sentences. Ensure each sentence expresses a single idea. Check transitions for smooth flow. Simplify complex sentences and remove redundancies. Replace vague words with precise vocabulary. Correct grammar and punctuation. Read aloud to confirm clarity and cohesion.

A4c) Identify subjects and verbs. Determine if subjects are singular or plural. Ensure verbs agree with their subjects. Consider compound subjects and intervening phrases. Correct mismatched verbs. Review sentences for grammatical accuracy.
