A10e) The social contract is the idea that individuals consent to surrender certain freedoms to authority in exchange for social order and protection.  
A11e) Phenomenology studies conscious experience from the first-person perspective, emphasizing structures and intentionality.  
A12e) Existentialism emphasizes human freedom, responsibility, and the search for meaning in an indifferent or absurd world.
